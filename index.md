# exomints_2026: Sprint projects from exomints 2026 conference

[exomints 2026](https://exomints.exoteric-lab.com/) was a workshop bringing together the exoplanet-atmospheres and statistical/ML-inference communities to make atmospheric “retrieval” robust, fast, and interpretable.

This repository is a place to openely collect sprint projects that happened during the meeting.
