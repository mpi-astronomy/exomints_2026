## Core themes

The sprints sit within the four themes of the workshop.

::::{grid} 1 1 2 2

:::{card} 01 · Simulation-based inference
SBI and other likelihood-free or hybrid methods for regimes where the likelihood is intractable or misspecified.
:::

:::{card} 02 · Surrogates & emulators
Neural and surrogate models that capture long-range wavelength correlations, standing in for expensive forward models.
:::

:::{card} 03 · GPU-accelerated & differentiable pipelines
Inference infrastructure that exploits modern hardware and gradients to explore high-dimensional, correlated parameter spaces.
:::

:::{card} 04 · Physics-informed, calibrated ML
Uncertainty-calibrated approaches that preserve interpretability rather than trading it away for speed.
:::

::::

## Outcomes

The workshop aims for concrete, citable deliverables:

- A community white paper and recommendation documents
- Shared benchmark problems and datasets
- A public software repository
